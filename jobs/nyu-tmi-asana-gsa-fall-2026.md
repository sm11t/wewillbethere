Position Overview
The Department of Technology Management and Innovation is seeking a highly organized Graduate Student Assistant with demonstrated experience using Asana to assist and support the department-wide implementation, workflow design, and optimization of the Asana project management platform. The ideal candidate will translate departmental processes into efficient Asana projects, forms, and automated workflows to streamline administrative, academic, and student service operations.

Key Responsibilities

Workflow & Project Architecture
- Assist with the building, configuring, and maintaining of custom Asana project boards, portfolios, timelines, and templates tailored to departmental needs (e.g., Department Administration, Student Services, Advising, and Faculty Requests).
- Assist with the design and implementation of custom fields, tags, dependencies, rules, and automated rules/workflows to increase administrative efficiency.

Forms & Process Standardization
- Build and manage Asana Forms for incoming requests (e.g., student inquiries, event support requests, faculty administrative tasks).
- Standardize process documentation and establish best practices for task intake, routing, and tracking.

System Migration & Maintenance
- Assist in migrating existing departmental spreadsheets, legacy databases, and tracking systems into organized Asana structures.
- Audit and maintain Asana workspace hygiene, project archives, and dashboard reporting.

Training & User Support
- Create quick-reference guides, video walk-throughs, or documentation for departmental staff and faculty.
- Provide onboarding support and basic troubleshooting for faculty, staff, and student workers adapting to the platform.
