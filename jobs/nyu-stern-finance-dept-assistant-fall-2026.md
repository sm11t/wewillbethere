Department Assistant - Finance Department
$18-30/hr. Onsite, based in New York City, NY. Work in person from the location. On campus.
Part-time, From September 21, 2026 to December 22, 2026. Work-Study eligible.
US work authorization required.

The Finance Department at NYU Stern seeks a Department Assistant for the Fall 2026 semester. This is an in-person position that offers an opportunity to gain hands-on experience supporting departmental operations and maintaining web content.

Responsibilities
The Department Assistant will provide support in two primary areas.
Office and administrative support: Assist with the day-to-day operations of the Finance Department office, including greeting and assisting visitors at the reception desk, answering basic questions, and performing administrative tasks such as copying and organizing materials.
Web support: Assist with web-related tasks, including updating and maintaining content on the Finance Department website.

Qualifications
HTML experience is required.
Strong organizational and communication skills.

How to Apply
Interested students should submit a resume and brief note of interest directly to Christina Borovilas at cb3972@stern.nyu.edu.
Please note that we typically receive a high volume of applications. Due to the volume of interest, we may only contact candidates selected for further consideration.
