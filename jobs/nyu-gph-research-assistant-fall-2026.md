Position Overview
The NYU School of Global Public Health (GPH) is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented graduate student to support research and scholarship within the Dean's Office. The Graduate Research Assistant will work closely with Dr. Bather and other faculty and researchers on projects designed to generate meaningful research findings and contribute to impactful public health scholarship.
This position provides an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in research, data analysis, literature reviews, academic writing, data visualization, survey development, and grant support while collaborating with faculty and staff across the School.

Responsibilities
The Graduate Research Assistant will:
Work up to 10 hours per week during the Fall 2026 semester.
Work directly with Dr. Bather and other researchers to support data analysis and research activities.
Conduct literature reviews and synthesize relevant research findings.
Develop clear summaries and written reports of research findings.
Create, edit, and format data visualizations, including tables, charts, and graphics.
Develop and maintain reference libraries using citation management software such as Zotero.
Collaborate with faculty and staff to develop and edit reports, manuscripts, abstracts, presentations, and other research materials.
Assist with the development of survey instruments using platforms such as Qualtrics, as well as other data collection tools such as Google Forms.
Support grant development activities, including preparing tables, figures, narratives, and other required materials.
Provide additional research and administrative support as assigned.

Qualifications
Current graduate student at NYU, preferably with coursework or experience in public health, research, data analysis, social sciences, or a related field.
Strong research, writing, editing, and organizational skills.
Experience conducting literature reviews and synthesizing research findings.
Familiarity with data analysis and data visualization.
Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.
Experience with Qualtrics, Zotero, Google Forms, or similar research tools is preferred.
Ability to work independently while also collaborating effectively with faculty, researchers, and staff.
Interest in public health research and scholarship is highly desirable.
Supervisor will meet with the incumbent once a week to review goals and expectations of this role.
