Job Description - Creative Technologist Graduate Assistant
The Center for Faculty Advancement (CFA) is working in partnership with a non-profit organization (CEI) on a creative media idea (film/ tv - digital content/ archival/ gaming) and is seeking up to 5 graduate students to support the exploration and prototyping of a new creative media platform. This platform is envisioned as a future-facing space that brings together creative projects, research, experimental media, and new approaches to distribution, centered on access and creator-led innovation.
This role is intentionally exploratory and prototype-driven, well-suited for graduate students interested in creative technology, media systems, platform thinking, and early-stage product or ecosystem design. Students will help translate a broad conceptual vision into concrete artifacts, such as a platform prototype, wireframes, user flows, and research summaries that can inform future development.

Role Overview
The Graduate Assistant(s) will report to the Director of Strategic Initiative and Community Engagement in CFA, Alexandria Meier, with direct support from CEI collaborators:
Bring front/back end skills to rapidly prototype and test adoption for consumers on a platform
Explore what a creator-centered, equity-driven platform could look like in practice
Drive early research, concept development, and prototyping efforts
Articulate how content, community, and distribution might interact within a single platform ecosystem
The work will culminate in early prototypes and concept materials (e.g., wireframes, diagrams, research briefs) suitable for internal review and future development.

Primary Responsibilities
Translate high-level ideas into wireframes, diagrams, and user journeys
Lead the development and continuous improvement of front-end and back-end platform capabilities, ensuring a seamless user experience supported by scalable, reliable, and efficient underlying systems.
Translate business and user needs into platform requirements and technical specifications, working across stakeholders and technical teams to prioritize and deliver new functionality.
Oversee the design and evolution of user-facing platform features and workflows, with a focus on usability, accessibility, responsiveness, and consistent experience across devices.
Guide the development and integration of back-end systems, APIs, data flows, and third-party services that support platform functionality and interoperability.
Assist in defining potential platform components (e.g., content hosting, archival materials, showcases, research libraries, creator tools)
Collaborate with NYU and CEI stakeholders in a highly iterative, feedback-driven process
Contribute to a short concept memo or prototype presentation outlining findings and recommendations

Preferred Skills & Background
Currently enrolled NYU graduate students (ITP, IMA, design, media studies, creative technology, CS, or related fields encouraged)
Interest and skills in developing platforms, creative technology, media ecosystems, or digital storytelling
Comfort working with ambiguity and early-stage ideas
Experience with or curiosity about wireframing, prototyping, systems thinking, or user-centered design; adept with technical requirements of the above
Strong synthesis and communication skills (visual, written, or both)
Familiarity with equity-driven or community-centered creative work is a plus, but not required

Time Commitment & Logistics
Up to 5 - 15 hours per week as work requires (flexible)
Sept. 2026 through May 2027 (potential extension- must reapply each semester)
Work may be remote or hybrid at the 411 Lafayette (Center for Faculty Advancement Office) or ITP

Learning Opportunities
Graduate Assistant(s) will gain experience in: Early-stage platform and ecosystem design; Translating creative vision into prototype-ready concepts; Working at the intersection of academia, industry, and nonprofit creative initiatives; Collaborative, interdisciplinary project development

To Apply
Please submit via Handshake: Resume; Short, 1 paragraph max, cover letter addressing: "What interests you about helping conceptualize and prototype a creative media platform and your technical skills in making something like this come to life?"

The individual interview will be with Alexandria Meier, and a member of the CEI team. The same interview questions will be used with all applications and will be behavioral/ exploratory.

Regular individual bi-weekly check-ins will be conducted as needed. Team meetings will take place every week in-person.
