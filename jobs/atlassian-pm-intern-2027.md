Working at Atlassian
Atlassians have the flexibility to work where they can collaborate most effectively with their team, whether at home, one of our global offices, or both. As a distributed-first company, all interviews are conducted virtually. We hire in any country where we have a legal entity. Learn how we work at go.atlassian.com/distributed.

Responsibilities
Your Future Org
Atlassian's Intern Program combines hands-on training, professional growth opportunities, mentorship, and the chance to work with innovative technologies like AI that are shaping the future of product management. This holistic approach empowers students to hit the ground running and sets them up for a successful and impactful career at Atlassian!

This role is located in the Pacific Time Zone. You must be willing and able to work out of this time zone to qualify.

This position is not eligible for F1 and J1 students. It is not available for any work sponsorship.

Your Future Team
From day one, you'll work with product, design, and engineering teams on problems that matter to customers. You'll speak with customers and partners, turn what you learn into practical next steps, and help write clear plans for new features. You'll research competitors and emerging AI tools, identify new product opportunities, and build prototypes to explore possible solutions. Along the way, you'll gain experience across different parts of product work and help design innovative solutions to the challenges our customers care about most.

What you'll bring
- Demonstrated interest in analyzing technology products, customer needs, and market dynamics
- Interest in translating customer insights into actionable outputs for engineering teams
- Interest or experience applying AI to improve product functionality and user experience
- Ability to consider multiple perspectives, including those of customers and development teams

Eligibility requirements
- Currently enrolled full time in a technical degree program and returning to the program after the internship, with expected graduation by June 2028
- Available to work a full-time internship schedule of 40 hours per week
