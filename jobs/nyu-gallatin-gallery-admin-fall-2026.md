New York University On Campus - Graduate Gallery Admin Assistant
Posted 4 days ago. Apply by October 2, 2026 at 11:59 PM
$30/hr. Hybrid, based in New York City, NY. Work in person for part of the week. On campus.
Part-time, 20 hours a week, From September 9, 2026 to December 16, 2026. Work-Study eligible.
US work authorization required. Open to candidates with OPT/CPT.

NYU Gallatin is seeking a graduate student to work with Rachel Plutzer (rp29@nyu.edu), the Senior Director of Special Events and Culture to assist with administrative management of the Gallatin Galleries. The Gallatin Galleries host 5 - 7 exhibits per year. Responsibilities include:
Serve as the liaison among the artist(s), the curator, and the Cultural Space staff to finalize exhibit timelines, staff scheduling, delivery dates, floor plans, and assemble lists of works;
Maintaining Asana projects;
Liaising with Gallatin staff and visiting artists;
Coordinating payments;
Arranging delivery and pick-up of art work;
Documenting work upon arrival and departure;
Contacting artists to determine scheduling for deliveries; preparing shipping labels and tracking where work is;
Arranging for messenger services or other means of delivery;
Handle logistical details for exhibitions including gathering the lists of works, handling insurance, payment agreements and filing paperwork, working with the appropriate staff on processing payments
Staff gallery events (opening receptions, talks, performances, etc).
Must be super organized and have an incredible attention for detail.
This position is hybrid and will require time on-site at Gallatin
Essential Skills: ability to work independently and know when to ask for help; creative problem solving; dependable and responsive; enthusiasm for the arts; effective multitasker. Familiarity with gallery management a plus.
