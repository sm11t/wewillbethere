## 2. What Asana is (researched 2026-09-08)

A work-management platform: a shared place where a team's work lives as
**tasks** inside **projects**, with fields, owners, dates and rules. The
vocabulary the posting uses, in plain terms:

- **Hierarchy:** organization → teams → projects → sections → tasks →
  subtasks. A task can live in several projects at once.
- **Views of the same project:** list, board (Kanban columns), timeline
  (Gantt), calendar. The posting's "boards" and "timelines" are these.
- **Custom fields:** typed columns on tasks - dropdowns, numbers, dates,
  people. "Custom fields are the primary lens through which Asana's
  reporting becomes useful" (search snippet, Work Management Hub 2026).
- **Tags, dependencies:** labels across projects; "task B waits on task A".
- **Rules:** if-this-then-that automation inside a project - when a task is
  added, set the assignee; when a field changes, move it to a section. 2026
  added AI-powered rules and a trigger for "collaborator added" (search
  snippets: Asana April and July 2026 release notes).
- **Forms:** a public or internal form whose submission becomes a task -
  the posting's "student inquiries, event support requests, faculty
  administrative tasks" are exactly this.
- **Portfolios:** a container over many projects with status roll-up; the
  posting's "Department Administration, Student Services, Advising, Faculty
  Requests" would each be a project, and the department a portfolio.
- **Dashboards and reporting:** charts over custom fields; "workspace
  hygiene" means archiving finished projects and keeping fields consistent.
- **Templates:** a saved project structure reused for each new instance.
- **AI Studio:** Asana's no-code AI workflow builder, on paid plans with a
  credit allotment; AI teammates; Smart Chat; an integration with Claude by
  Anthropic (search snippets, 2026 guides).

**Plans - verified on https://asana.com/pricing, 2026-09-08:** Personal is
free for 2 users with unlimited tasks and projects but **no custom fields,
rules, forms, timeline, portfolios or AI Studio**. Starter ($10.99/user/month
annual) adds custom fields, rules ("unlimited automations"), forms and AI
Studio Basic. Advanced ($24.99) adds timeline/Gantt, portfolios and
formulas. The pricing page does not state a trial length; a trial exists
per the sign-up flow (not verified). NYU likely holds an organizational
license if a department is rolling it out; ask in the interview whether
student workers get a seat.

**Asana Academy - verified on https://academy.asana.com/, 2026-09-08:** free,
self-paced. Learning paths: Team leader (3 courses), Team member (2), IT/Asana
admin (3), Creating basic workflows (3), 6 steps to rolling out Asana to your
team (6). Skill badges and certificates: **Asana Foundations Skill Badge** (6
courses), **Workflow Specialist Certificate** (2 courses, advanced), **Asana
Administrator Certificate** (13 courses, advanced), AI Studio Foundations
(about 3 hours), AI for Work, Campaign Management, Resource Management.
Courses named: "Getting started with Asana in 15 minutes or less",
"Automating workflows in Asana" (45-90 min), "Building agentic workflows
with Asana" (60 min, live). The two that match this posting exactly are the
Workflow Specialist Certificate and the IT/Asana admin path.
