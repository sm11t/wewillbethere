Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Waymo's Systems Engineering team works together to blend software and hardware systems in groundbreaking new ways. We set the high performance standards that ensure our vehicles run smoothly and keep passengers safe, then design and perform the tests that validate that performance.

## You will

- Design and implement metrics, test plans, and simulated scenarios for testing the autonomous vehicle in off-nominal situations, such as after a fault occurs or when remote assistance is needed, across multiple platforms and in support of rapid commercialization
- Create dashboards to monitor, visualize, and analyze fleet-wide autonomous vehicle fault rates on public roads
- Support the Verification and Validation (V&V) of Fault Protection and Remote Assistance behaviors.
- Build automated pipelines using Python and SQL to update, version, and validate test coverage across safety-critical driving scenarios.

## You have

- Currently enrolled in a MS program in an engineering, computer science, or a related technical field of study
- Enthusiasm to understand a complex system and its various components
- Experience with data analysis/modeling using Python and SQL

## We prefer

- Familiarity with autonomous vehicle technology and systems engineering principles.
- Comfortable with ambiguous problems in a fast paced environment.
