General Research Assistant
Posted 3 days ago. Apply by December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM
$30/hr. Onsite, based in New York City, NY. Work in person from the location. On campus.
Part-time, From September 3, 2026 to December 31, 2026. Work-Study eligible.
US work authorization required.

A Research Assistant (URA) will work with a faculty member, lab, research center, and/or other organization at NYU on an on-going research project. A student hired for this position might:
Aid in data collection (e.g. participate in the running of interviews or focus groups; contribute to survey design or administration; run lab-based experiments; recruit participants);
Conduct parts of a literature review, searching for previously published studies of interest to the current project;
Collaborate on sharing the research findings by supporting the writing of articles, presentations, social media posts, etc..
Other opportunities may be available depending on particular research projects.

Special note: this is a general job description for Research Assistants, and NYU needs multiple Graduate RAs in a variety of settings across campus every semester. As such, please describe in your cover letter not only your school and your (planned) course of study but also any subject areas you have particular interest or expertise in. For instance, if you write that you are a CAS Sophomore, majoring in Chemistry, minoring in Journalism, with an interest in cities (because you're from Houston) and experience in education (because you tutored elementary school students your first year at NYU)...that could allow the Director, Undergraduate Research to pass your name along to any faculty or research centers involved in Chemistry, Journalism, Urban Planning/Policy, or Education. Similarly, if you have particular skills which you think could be helpful in a research setting, you should note them (previous faculty requests have covered everything from fluency in specific languages to familiarity with computer programming to experience with data to dexterity with film editing). Note: this is a general application pool that will be drawn from for particular research assistant roles that are requested of the Center for Undergraduate Research. Applicants will only hear about next steps if they are contacted by a potential supervisor for an interview.

Qualifications - Required
Currently enrolled graduate student at NYU
Desire to learn more about research
Interest in particular topics or fields (please describe your varied interests in your cover letter)

Remit and Compensation
This is a temporary, project based appointment with a flexible start date and an end date as late as August 2025, if needed. When matched with a research project, the weekly workflow will be established based on hiring need and student availability, but can be as few as 5 hours per week and no more than 20; during the winter and summer breaks, students not taking courses can work up to 35 hours/week. Bi-weekly check-in meetings will be conducted with the hiring supervisor to evaluate performance. This is an on-campus position.

NYC Pay Transparency: the hourly base salary range for student employment positions are $17-$18 per hour for undergraduate students and $30 per hour for graduate students. The hourly rate for graduate employees is based on the collective bargaining agreement with Local 7902.
Federal Work Study is not required for this role, unless specifically stated within the job description.
