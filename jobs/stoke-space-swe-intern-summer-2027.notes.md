# Stoke Space — Summer 2027 Software Intern (Boltline)

## READ THIS BEFORE SPENDING EFFORT

**Export control is the gate, and it is not the usual sponsorship question.**

Stoke Space builds orbital launch vehicles. That work is controlled under ITAR
and the EAR, and US launch companies almost universally restrict these roles to
"US persons" — citizens, permanent residents, asylees and refugees. A US person
requirement is not a preference an employer can waive for a strong candidate;
it is a legal restriction on who may access the technical data.

The posting text as pasted does **not** state this. But it is close to standard
across the industry, so **check the full posting and the application form before
investing time.** If Asmit is on an F-1 visa, this role and most of Stoke's
software roles are likely closed regardless of fit — which would be a shame,
because on the technical merits this is the strongest match he has seen.

This is a harder gate than McKesson's. Sponsorship is a cost an employer
chooses not to pay; export control is a rule they cannot break.
